Core Capabilities

Everything the department needs, built in.

From faculty availability collection to final timetable distribution. CCISched handles the entire scheduling cycle in one centralized platform.

Machine Learning

Random Forest predicts optimal faculty-to-course assignments from historical patterns. CP-SAT then enforces every constraint mathematically guaranteeing a feasible, conflict-free output.

Automated Timetable Generation

The system plots available time slots, room assignments, and faculty workloads automatically eliminating manual schedule plotting entirely.

Real-Time Conflict Detection

Overlapping schedules among faculty, courses, time slots, and rooms are flagged and blocked instantly before they reach the timetable.

Faculty Workload Balancing

Teaching loads are distributed equitably across full-time and part-time faculty, respecting contract limits and institutional policies.

Centralized Dashboard

Monitor schedules, track faculty availability, and manage course offerings from one clean interface filterable by section, faculty, room, or time slot.

One-Click Distribution

Once finalized, the timetable is pushed directly to individual faculty dashboards with a single button no emails, no spreadsheets.

The Pipeline

From input to optimized schedule in minutes.

01

Collect Faculty Availability

Faculty members submit their preferred teaching days and time windows through their personal dashboards. The Chairperson can view all availability records in real time.

02

Manage Course Offerings

The Chairperson configures the course list codes, units, year levels, room types, and section counts for the upcoming semester.

03

Generate Faculty Assignments

The Random Forest classifier analyzes historical patterns and faculty profiles to predict the optimal faculty-to-course mapping. Results are displayed for review.

04

Optimize & Generate Timetable

The CP-SAT solver enforces all hard constraints no double-booking, load limits, room capacities producing a conflict-free timetable that can be exported as PDF.

05

Distribute to Faculty

One click pushes the finalized schedule to every faculty member's dashboard. Faculty see only their own timetable; the Chairperson sees the master view.

About the Project

Built for PUP-CCIS. Designed to last.

CCISched is a capstone research project developed at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines — College of Computer and Information Sciences.

CCISched addresses the ineffectiveness of manual academic scheduling by automating faculty assignment and timetable optimization through a hybrid intelligent approach combining a Random Forest classifier with a Constraint Programming (CP-SAT) solver.

The system resolves multi-variable scheduling constraints — faculty availability, teaching load limits, course offerings, and room allocation — that are impossible to manage reliably by hand at institutional scale.

Its quality is validated against the ISO 25010:2023 standard, ensuring functional suitability, usability, security, and reliability across all administrative workflows.
